👋 Introduction

Welcome to Chef's Pages ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our recipe management and meal planning application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. Chef's Pages is designed with privacy in mind—your recipes, wines, and collections are yours, and we believe in minimal data collection.

Our Privacy Commitment: Chef's Pages is built to work primarily on your device. We do not use analytics or tracking services, and we only transmit your data when you explicitly choose to share content with others.

Age Requirement: Chef's Pages is intended for users aged 18 and older due to wine cellar and alcohol-related features. By using this app, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age.

💾 How Your Data is Stored

Local Storage

All your recipes, wines, collections, and other content are stored locally on your device in a secure database. This data remains on your device and is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID).

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled for Chef's Pages, your data will sync across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your private iCloud container, meaning:

  • Only you can access this data through your Apple ID
  • The data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We cannot access your synced data
  • Apple's privacy policies govern iCloud data storage

Keychain Storage

Your anonymous user ID is stored securely in iOS Keychain, Apple's secure storage system for sensitive information. This ensures the ID persists across app reinstalls while remaining protected.

Automatic Backups

Chef's Pages creates automatic local backups of your data to protect against data loss. These backups are stored on your device and follow a retention policy that keeps the most recent backups.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve the Chef's Pages experience:

  • Core Functionality: To store and display your recipes, wines, collections, shopping lists, and menu plans
  • Cross-Device Sync: To synchronize your content across your Apple devices via iCloud

🤖 On-Device Processing

Chef's Pages uses machine learning to enhance your experience. Importantly, all machine learning processing happens entirely on your device:

Wine Profile Prediction

Our CoreML models analyze wine characteristics (varietal, region, etc.) to predict tasting profiles (sweetness, acidity, body, tannin levels). This processing:

  • Happens entirely on your iPhone
  • Does not send any data to external servers
  • Uses models stored within the app

Voice-to-Recipe Transcription

Using Apple's on-device speech recognition and language models, you can dictate recipes using your voice. This feature:

  • Processes your voice entirely on-device
  • Does not transmit audio to any external service
  • Requires iOS 18.2 or later for full functionality

Recipe Generation and Improvement

On-device language models can help generate and improve recipes. All processing occurs locally on your device with no external API calls.

AI Accuracy Notice

Important: AI-powered features are provided for guidance and convenience only. AI systems can and do make mistakes. All AI-generated content—including wine predictions, transcribed recipes, and recipe suggestions—should be treated as helpful starting points, not as verified or authoritative information.

Always review and verify AI outputs before use, especially for food safety, allergens, and dietary requirements. For complete terms regarding AI limitations, please see our Terms and Conditions.
